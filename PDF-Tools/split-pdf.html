How to Split and Extract PDF Pages Securely Online

Extracting specific chapters, splitting large multi-page reports, or isolating individual pages from confidential documents is a frequent requirement. Conventional online PDF utilities require uploading sensitive documents to external remote servers, posing severe data privacy and security vulnerabilities.

Our advanced **Client-Side PDF Splitter** resolves this dilemma by running entirely inside your web browser. Leveraging modern browser technologies like `pdf.js` and `pdf-lib`, all document rendering, visual thumbnail previews, page rotation, and splitting occur locally on your machine. Your private files never leave your device.

Advanced Features Built for Maximum Productivity

  • Interactive Visual Thumbnail Grid: Inspect every page of your PDF instantly and click to select or deselect pages visually without guessing page numbers.
  • Smart Split Modes: Combine selected pages into a single custom document, extract each page into separate files packaged into a `.zip` archive, or split automatically by interval chunks.
  • Page Rotation & Watermarks: Correct inverted or sideways pages instantly with 90-degree rotation adjustments and stamp professional text watermarks across exported sheets.
  • 100% Client-Side Privacy: Zero server uploads guarantee absolute data confidentiality for corporate records, legal paperwork, and personal receipts.
