How to Add or Remove Watermarks in PDF Files Securely

Protecting intellectual property with custom "CONFIDENTIAL", "DRAFT", or corporate logo watermarks is essential for business documents. Similarly, cleaning or removing unwanted draft stamps is frequently required before publishing final reports. Traditional online tools require uploading private contracts and reports to remote cloud servers, creating security risks.

Our advanced **Client-Side PDF Watermark Utility** processes your files locally right inside your web browser. Using high-performance JavaScript engines (`pdf-lib` and `PDF.js`), text or image stamps are embedded or filtered out directly on your machine without any external data transmission.

Key Features of Our Watermark Utility

  • Dual Mode Operation: Easily switch between adding customized watermarks and cleaning/sanitizing unwanted text stamps.
  • Text & Image Support: Stamp customized text with custom color, font size, and rotation, or overlay corporate PNG/JPG logos.
  • Flexible Positioning: Place watermarks diagonally across the center or anchor them to any page corner.
  • 100% Privacy & Security: Total browser-based execution guarantees your sensitive documents never leave your device.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Upload PDF File: Drag and drop your .pdf document into the secure upload dropzone or select a file from your device.
  2. Choose Mode & Options: Select either **Add Watermark** or **Remove Watermark** and configure your styling or cleaning parameters.
  3. Process & Download: Click the process button to compile and download your modified document instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a watermark to a PDF document?

Upload your PDF file, choose the Add Watermark mode, configure text or image options, adjust position, opacity, and rotation, then click Process & Download.

Can I remove watermarks or clean text layers from my PDF?

Yes! Switch to the Remove / Clean Watermark mode to flatten, sanitize, or filter out specific textual overlays from your PDF document pages.
