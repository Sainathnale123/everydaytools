How to Extract Text from Scanned PDFs Online Using Browser OCR

Traditional PDF documents containing pure text can be easily queried or copied. However, scanned contracts, receipts, and paper forms saved as image-based PDFs cannot be searched or edited directly. Conventional online tools require uploading sensitive personal or corporate documents to external servers, creating potential privacy risks.

Our advanced **Client-Side PDF OCR & Text Extraction Tool** performs optical character recognition entirely within your web browser. Powered by `PDF.js` and `Tesseract.js`, your PDF pages are rendered locally into high-resolution images and scanned for character patterns without transmitting data across the internet.

Key Features of Our PDF OCR Utility

  • Client-Side Processing: Complete text extraction runs locally on your device for absolute privacy and security.
  • Multi-Language Support: Accurately recognize text across major global languages including English, Spanish, French, and German.
  • Instant Export Options: Easily copy extracted text to your clipboard or download it as a formatted text document.
  • Zero Installation Required: Works directly in any modern desktop or mobile browser without plugins.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Upload Scanned PDF: Drag and drop your image-based PDF document into the secure upload dropzone or browse from your device.
  2. Select Language: Choose the primary language of your document from the options menu to enhance OCR accuracy.
  3. Extract & Save: Wait for the local OCR engine to process your document, then copy your text or download it as a .txt file.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does PDF OCR work in the browser?

Your scanned PDF pages are rendered locally into high-resolution images using PDF.js, and Tesseract.js scans them to extract text without uploading files to any server.

Can I copy or download the extracted text?

Yes! Once the OCR process finishes, you can directly copy the text to your clipboard or download it as a .txt file.
