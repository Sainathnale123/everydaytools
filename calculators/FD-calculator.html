Understanding Fixed Deposit (FD) Compounding & Investment Yields

A Fixed Deposit (FD) is a secure financial instrument offered by banks and non-banking financial companies (NBFCs) providing investors a higher rate of interest than standard savings accounts over a fixed tenure. The core financial mechanic relies on compound interest, where earned interest is reinvested periodically to accelerate wealth generation.

Compound Interest Formula Explained

The compounding maturity amount is calculated using the standard mathematical formula:

$$A = P \left(1 + \frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt}$$

Where A is the total maturity value, P is the principal investment sum, r is the annual nominal interest rate (decimal), n represents the compounding frequency per year, and t signifies the tenure duration in years.
