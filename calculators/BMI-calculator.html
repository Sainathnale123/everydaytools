Advanced Body Composition Analysis: BMI, Navy Fat Percentage & Waist-to-Height Ratio

Evaluating overall metabolic health requires looking beyond standard Body Mass Index (BMI). While BMI provides a rapid screening threshold for height-to-weight proportions, combining it with the U.S. Navy Body Fat Formula and Waist-to-Height Ratio (WHtR) offers a precise clinical picture of visceral adiposity and lean mass distribution.

Why Track Waist-to-Height Ratio (WHtR)?

Research indicates that keeping your waist circumference to less than half your height (WHtR < 0.5) significantly reduces the likelihood of metabolic syndrome, Type 2 diabetes, and cardiovascular complications, regardless of total body weight.
