Understanding Percentages: Formulas, Applications, and Manual Calculations

A percentage is a dimensionless number representing a fraction of 100. Derived from the Latin term per centum (meaning "by the hundred"), percentages are universal across financial analysis, academic grading, statistical research, and commercial discounts.

Core Percentage Formulas

  • Finding a percentage of a number: $(X / 100) \times Y$
  • Calculating what percentage one number is of another: $(X / Y) \times 100$
  • Percentage Increase: $[(NewValue - InitialValue) / InitialValue] \times 100$
