Understanding Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) & Metabolic Science

Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) represents the absolute minimum amount of energy (measured in calories) your body requires to maintain basic life-sustaining functions while at complete rest. These mandatory physiological processes include respiration, blood circulation, cellular synthesis, and neurological function.

Scientifically Validated BMR Formulas

Different computational models evaluate body mass and dimensions differently:

$$\text{Mifflin-St Jeor (Male)} = (10 \times \text{weight in kg}) + (6.25 \times \text{height in cm}) - (5 \times \text{age}) + 5$$

For individuals with known body fat percentages, the Katch-McArdle Formula estimates metabolic rate strictly based on Lean Body Mass (LBM), making it exceptionally reliable for athletes and resistance trainers.
