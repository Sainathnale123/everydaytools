Mastering Mutual Funds with Step-Up SIPs & Goal Planning

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) empowers retail investors to commit fixed amounts regularly into equity or debt mutual funds. Utilizing rupee-cost averaging eliminates the hazards of market timing, while compound interest generates exponential long-term returns.

Why Use an Annual Step-Up SIP?

As your career and salary progress, maintaining a static monthly SIP amount limits your compounding potential. A **Step-Up SIP** scales your monthly contribution by a chosen percentage every year, letting your investments keep pace with career growth and effortlessly hitting retirement milestones decades sooner.
