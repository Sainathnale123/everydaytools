Financial & Investment Calculators

Financial calculations are an important part of planning loans, investments, savings, and personal finances. Everyday Tools provides several financial calculators that can help users understand estimated payments, returns, interest, taxes, and investment values.

The EMI Calculator helps estimate monthly loan payments using the principal amount, interest rate, and loan tenure. It can be useful when comparing different loan amounts and repayment periods. The result can also help users understand the estimated total interest payable over the selected tenure.

The SIP Calculator can be used to estimate the potential future value of regular investments. Users can enter their monthly investment, expected annual return, and investment duration to understand how their contributions may grow over time. Actual investment returns can vary, and calculator results should be treated as estimates rather than guaranteed returns.

The Fixed Deposit (FD) Calculator helps estimate the maturity amount and interest earned on a fixed deposit based on the deposit amount, interest rate, and investment period. The Simple Interest Calculator and Compound Interest Calculator provide quick estimates for different interest calculations and can be useful for learning how interest accumulates over time.

The CAGR Calculator helps calculate the Compound Annual Growth Rate between an initial investment value and a final value over a specified period. CAGR is commonly used to understand the annualized growth rate of an investment or other financial value.

The Brokerage Calculator can help users estimate brokerage-related charges based on the information entered. Brokerage structures and applicable charges can vary between brokers, exchanges, transaction types, and account plans, so users should always verify the final charges with their broker.

The GST Calculator helps calculate Goods and Services Tax amounts and can be useful for determining GST-inclusive and GST-exclusive prices. Users can enter the applicable GST rate and amount to estimate the tax component and resulting price.

Health & Wellness Calculators

Everyday Tools also provides calculators for commonly used health and wellness measurements. These tools are intended to provide general informational estimates and should not be considered medical diagnosis or treatment tools.

The Body Mass Index (BMI) Calculator estimates BMI using height and weight. BMI is commonly used as a general screening measurement for comparing body weight relative to height, but it does not directly measure body fat or provide a complete assessment of an individual's health.
