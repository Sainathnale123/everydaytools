Mastering Your Loan: EMI, Prepayments & Refinancing

An Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is structured so that initial installments prioritize interest obligations over principal clearance. Leveraging partial prepayments during early loan years directly slashes outstanding principal, drastically cutting overall interest expense.
