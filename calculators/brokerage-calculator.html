Every discount broker or full-service broker implements distinct commission structures. This calculator gives you full flexibility to input your exact broker fee structure—whether you pay flat per-order fees (like ₹20 per executed buy/sell order) or percentage-based commission models—while accurately computing standard statutory taxes including STT, exchange transaction fees, SEBI charges, and state stamp duty.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I customize my broker fee?

You can choose your brokerage mode between Flat Fee per order, Percentage of total turnover, or Zero Brokerage, and enter your exact rate to instantly update all calculations.

Are taxes calculated based on standard exchange rules?

Yes, government levies such as STT, Goods and Services Tax (GST), SEBI turnover fees, and state stamp duty are calculated according to current NSE and BSE statutory guidelines.
