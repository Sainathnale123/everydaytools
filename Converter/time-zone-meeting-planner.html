UTC, GMT and Daylight Saving Time Explained

UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) provides a common reference for calculating international time differences. GMT (Greenwich Mean Time) is another widely recognized time standard, particularly when discussing UK time.

Some countries and regions change their clocks during the year because of Daylight Saving Time (DST). This means that the difference between two locations can change depending on the selected date. Always use the actual meeting date when planning an international call.

Why Time Zone Meeting Planning Is Important
