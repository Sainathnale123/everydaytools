Express Creativity with an Online Drawing Canvas

Unleash your imagination using our lightweight browser-based drawing pad. Whether you are sketching quick concepts, illustrating ideas, or letting kids practice hand-eye coordination with digital doodles, this tool provides a responsive workspace.

Features & Capabilities

Customize your experience with adjustable brush sizes, a color picker, an eraser mode, and instant image downloading. No downloads or installations required—draw directly inside any modern web browser on desktop or mobile devices.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save my drawing?

Click the "Save Image" button above the canvas to instantly download your artwork as a PNG file to your device.

Can I use the drawing pad on my phone or tablet?

Yes! The canvas fully supports touch gestures, allowing you to draw smoothly using your finger or a stylus on mobile devices.

How do I switch to the eraser?

Simply click the "Eraser" button in the toolbar. To switch back to drawing, click the "Brush" button and select your desired color.
