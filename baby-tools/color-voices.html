Frequently Asked Questions

How do I hear the color name?

Simply tap or click on any card in the grid to trigger text-to-speech audio pronunciation of the color's name.

Can I filter colors by category?

Yes! Use the filter buttons at the top of the page to instantly switch between primary, warm, cool, and neutral colors.

Is this color explorer free?

Yes, all features, search filters, and audio pronunciations are 100% free to use directly inside your browser.
