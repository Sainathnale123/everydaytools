Frequently Asked Questions

Simply tap or click on any card in the grid to trigger text-to-speech audio pronunciation of the item's name.

Can I filter items by category?

Yes! Use the filter buttons at the top of the page to instantly switch between viewing all items, only fruits, or only vegetables.
