Frequently Asked Questions

How do I hear the animal name?

Simply tap or click on any card in the grid to trigger text-to-speech audio pronunciation of the animal's name.

Can I filter animals by habitat?

Yes! Use the filter buttons at the top of the page to instantly switch between farm animals, wild safari species, forest creatures, and marine life.

Is this animal explorer free?

Yes, all features, search filters, and audio pronunciations are 100% free to use directly inside your browser.
