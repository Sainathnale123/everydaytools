Explore the alphabet and phonics! Tap any card to hear clear letter pronunciations and words.

Master Phonics and Alphabet Sounds Interactively

Help young learners discover the alphabet with our A to Z Voices explorer. Each card features an uppercase and lowercase letter, an accompanying vocabulary example, and instant audio speech synthesis to reinforce phonetic learning and letter recognition.

Early Childhood Literacy & Pronunciation

Interactive audio tools accelerate early reading skills. By listening to slow and clear letter sounds paired with familiar words, children can easily connect letters to their spoken sounds in an engaging, self-paced browser environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I hear the letter sound?

Simply tap or click on any letter card to hear both the letter name and its example word spoken aloud.

Can I filter between vowels and consonants?

Yes! Use the filter buttons at the top to toggle between Vowels, Consonants, or view the complete A to Z set.

Is this alphabet tool free?

Yes, all features and audio pronunciations are 100% free to use directly in your web browser.
