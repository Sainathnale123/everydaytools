Frequently Asked Questions

How do I hear the name of the body part?

Simply tap or click on any card in the grid to trigger text-to-speech audio pronunciation of the body part's name.

Can I filter parts by region?

Yes! Use the filter buttons at the top of the page to instantly switch between head and face, upper body, lower body, and internal organs.

Is this body parts explorer free?

Yes, all features, search filters, and audio pronunciations are 100% free to use directly inside your browser.
