Privacy Policy

Your privacy is paramount. Learn how we protect your information when using Everyday Tools.

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Everyday Tools (accessible from everydaytools.co.in). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by Everyday Tools and how we use it.

2. Client-Side Processing (No Data Storage)

The vast majority of utilities on Everyday Tools (including calculators, converters, and generators) execute logic entirely within your web browser using JavaScript. We do not transmit, collect, or store any sensitive inputs, dates, or numerical data entered into our calculation tools on our servers.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Everyday Tools uses "cookies". These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the user experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type or other information.

4. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & AdSense

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to everydaytools.co.in and other sites on the internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.

5. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to analyze traffic and usage patterns on our website. Google Analytics uses cookies to gather non-personally identifiable information such as IP addresses, device types, browser types, and pages visited. This helps us optimize website performance and improve user experience.

6. Third Party Privacy Policies

Everyday Tools's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information.

7. GDPR & CCPA Privacy Rights

Under applicable data privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA), users retain rights regarding personal information request disclosures or deletions. As Everyday Tools does not collect personal user data through account creation, your usage remains completely anonymous.

8. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

9. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to reach out via our Contact Page.
